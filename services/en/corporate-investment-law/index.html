FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Firm assist foreign investors with company formation?

Yes. Foreign-investor incorporation can be assessed by reviewing nationality, business activity, applicable restrictions and any special approvals, then identifying the required documents and procedures.

Does the service include post-incorporation amendments?

Yes. Services may include amendments to constitutional documents, management, capital, objects, registered office or ownership structure, subject to the company form and competent authority requirements.

Can the Firm advise on the appropriate legal form?

The appropriate legal form depends on the activity, number of founders, liability preferences, capital, funding plan, management, tax and regulatory obligations, and anticipated growth.

Can an investor based in the Gulf start a company formation process in Egypt?

The proposed business activity, legal form and initial document requirements can be reviewed remotely. Incorporation steps, signatures, powers of attorney and any attendance depend on the chosen entity, activity and the requirements of the competent authorities.
